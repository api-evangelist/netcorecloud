# Netcore

> Agentic Marketing Platform for increased conversions, engagement, and personalization.

Netcore.ai offers an Agentic Marketing Platform designed to help enterprise and mid-market businesses, particularly in e-commerce, retail, BFSI, and travel, enhance customer engagement and drive conversions. The platform leverages AI to personalize user experiences across multiple channels, including email, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS. It aims to improve key metrics such as user engagement, conversion rates, and customer retention.

The core value proposition lies in its ability to deliver hyper-personalized customer journeys through an AI-driven approach. This enables businesses to understand their customers better via a Customer Data Platform (CDP) and then act on those insights using a suite of communication tools. The platform is suitable for businesses of all sizes looking to optimize their marketing efforts and achieve measurable growth. Key use cases include increasing ROI, improving product discovery, driving policy renewals, and enhancing overall customer lifetime value.
